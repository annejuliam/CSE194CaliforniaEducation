# Resources and interpretation guide

Links checked or located in official sources on November 22, 2026. Dates identify
publication/data periods; n.d. means no publication date is asserted. A listed
resource is not necessarily data ingested by the app. This bibliography combines
the team's earlier collection with methods and policy context.

## Data used in the comparison

1. **California Department of Education (CDE). Public Schools and Districts Data
   Files / Public School Directory.** Snapshot: October 29, 2026.
   [Source](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/si/ds/pubschls.asp).
   Supplies CDS identifiers and coordinates. The pilot excludes charter and
   alternative schools and other districts. Points are not attendance boundaries.
2. **CDE. Free or Reduced-Price Meal (Student Poverty) Data.** 2024–25.
   [Definitions](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/ad/filessp.asp) and
   [workbook](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/ad/documents/frpm2425.xlsx).
   Supplies K–12 enrollment and eligibility counts. Eligibility is not household
   income, individual poverty, or meal usage. Page and workbook are one source.
3. **CDE. Graduation Rate Indicator: Data Files.** 2025 Dashboard.
   [Source](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/cm/graddatafiles.asp).
   Supplies school-level ALL-group combined four-/five-year graduation values.
   Its denominator differs from current enrollment.

## Definitions and responsible comparisons

4. **CDE. Graduation Rate Indicator: File Structure.** n.d.; accessed November 22,
   2026. [Definitions](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/AC/cm/fsgrad.asp).
   Supports correct indicator labeling. This is methodology for source 3, not
   an additional empirical dataset.
5. **U.S. Census Bureau. Comparing ACS Data.** n.d.; accessed November 22, 2026.
   [Guidance](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/guidance/comparing-acs-data.html).
   ACS estimates have margins of error. The guidance distinguishes comparable
   periods and advises against comparing overlapping five-year estimates.
   Relevant to a future income extension; no ACS data is in the current pilot.

## Research and statewide context

6. **Kurlaender, M., Reardon, S., Kalogrides, D., Reed, S., Brighouse, H., and
   Reber, S. Outcomes and Demographics of California's Schools.** Policy Analysis
   for California Education, September 2018.
   [Policy brief](https://edpolicyinca.org/publications/outcomes-and-demographics-californias-schools).
   Documents demographic and socioeconomic differences in educational
   opportunities and outcomes. Historical context, not current pilot values
   or proof of a single causal explanation.
7. **CDE. Fingertip Facts on Education in California.** 2025–26 or most recent
   available year; reviewed October 15, 2026.
   [Statewide overview](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/ad/ceffingertipfacts.asp).
   Enrollment and school-type context; statewide totals are not school records.
8. **CDE. Local Control Funding Formula Overview.** n.d.; accessed November 22,
   2026. [Funding context](https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/lcffoverview.asp).
   Describes base, supplemental, and concentration funding. Future resource
   analysis should examine funding rules and actual expenditures rather than
   equating local income/property tax with a school's budget. No budget values
   are displayed. Official indexed content was available; direct automated
   access encountered a bot challenge.

## Existing tools and limits of novelty

9. **Ed-Data Education Data Partnership. Fiscal, Demographic, and Performance
   Data on California's K–12 Schools.** Continuously updated; accessed November
   22, 2026. [Ed-Data](https://ed-data.org/).
   Already supports school/district comparisons. A comparison table alone is
   not novel; the final project must demonstrate specific added value.
10. **Educational Opportunity Project at Stanford University. The 2009–2019
    Educational Opportunity Explorer.** Data: 2008–09 through 2018–19.
    [Explorer](https://edopportunity.org/opportunity/explorer/).
    Uses public-school test scores in grades 3–8; its years and grades differ
    from this high-school pilot. Used as research and design context only.
11. **CDE. California School Dashboard.** Continuously updated; accessed November
    22, 2026. [Dashboard](https://www.caschooldashboard.org/).
    An existing public accountability interface. The pilot uses downloadable CDE
    graduation data, not scraped displays. Its interactive site needs JavaScript.

## Interpretation

Keep enrollment, meal eligibility, and graduation separate, not a composite
quality score. Preserve source/year labels and geographic scope in presentations.
Household income, property tax, spending, and test scores are not in the current
comparison. Adding them requires explicit geography, period, population, and
missing/suppressed-value handling.

The sources must meaningfully support the final work. Eleven bibliography entries
are not eleven independent datasets, and a link count alone does not establish
depth, accuracy, or a full research score.
