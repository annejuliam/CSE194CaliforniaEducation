Does Where You Live Shape Your Educational?


Education plays an important role in shaping a student's future, but not every student has access to the same opportunities. Across California, public schools serve communities with vastly different income levels, resources, and educational outcomes. Students from lower-income communities may face challenges that affect their academic experiences, while students in wealthier areas may have access to additional resources and support.


Our project aims to explore these differences through an interactive map of California's public schools. By comparing household income, school performance, student demographics, and educational resources, we hope to make information about educational inequality more accessible to the public. Rather than drawing conclusions for our users, we want to provide reliable data that encourages people to explore, ask questions, and better understand how socioeconomic factors may influence educational opportunities.
